Straight answers

The questions worth asking.

Is this just public data repackaged?

Yes — and that's the point. Every permit is public. What isn't public is the answer to "which of these 2,600 filings this week has my trade slot still open." That answer is the product. You're paying for the reading, not the records.

What if my trade is already listed on most jobs?

Then we filter those out, which is the entire value. You only receive jobs where your trade is genuinely absent from the filing. If a market turns out to have no open slots, we tell you before you pay — and don't take the subscription.

How current is it?

City feeds publish daily. The Monday brief covers filings from the previous seven days, with anything older clearly dated so you know what you're walking into.

Can I get my money back?

First brief is free. After that, cancel any time and the current month is refunded if you tell us within seven days that the leads weren't relevant.

Do you sell the same list to my competitor?

No. One trade, one metro, one subscriber. If a slot is taken, the listing closes.
